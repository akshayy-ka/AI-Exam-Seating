AI Search Heuristics (CSP Optimization & Demonstration)

Configure intelligent variable ordering and constraint propagation in the backtracking engine. Toggle these during demonstration to compare search steps and performance.

Fail-First Principle: Selects the unassigned student with the fewest legal seats left in their domain. Resolves tightly constrained students early, pruning dead ends near the root of the search tree.

Constraint Propagation: Immediately removes assigned seats from all students, and removes adjacent seats from students taking the same exam paper. Triggers instant backtracking upon Domain Wipeout (0 seats left).

🎓 Examiner & Viva Demonstration Guide

1. Why Backtracking with CSP? Brute-force generates \(O(D^N)\) states, which explodes with real examination classes. CSP models each student as variable \(X_i\) and seats as domain \(D_i\). Backtracking performs a systematic Depth-First Search with early pruning.

2. Demonstrating MRV: If a student has special room restrictions or fewer available seats, MRV chooses them first: \(\arg\min_{S} |D(S)|\). If they cannot be seated, the solver fails immediately without wasting millions of combinations on unconstrained students.

3. Demonstrating Forward Checking: Without FC, standard backtracking places a student and only checks consistency with already-assigned seats. With FC, every placement looks 1 step ahead and removes invalid seats from future students, pruning search branches before recursing.

4. Live Demonstration Tip: On a dense seating problem, compare the step count with MRV & FC enabled vs. disabled to highlight algorithmic speedup!
